# Mayhem
A Balatro content mod designed around incremental progression inside one run. 

Mayhem is an expansive content mod which, among many others, introduces a fusion system. 2 specific Jokers can be combined into a new Joker that is much greater than the sum of its parts, as long as all of the prerequisites have been met. This is done multiple times, gradually upgrading your Joker and reaching a new magnitude of incomprehensible numbers with each fusion. 

The Blinds will also catch up eventually and fusing is not exactly easy. 

This mod turns Balatro on its head. This is no longer about reaching Ante 8 and winning the run, your goal is now to become as strong as possible. Many systems are set in place to make the progression incremental, there will keep being a "next upgrade" for a very long time.

# EPILEPSY AND AUDIO WARNING
Mayhem has a bunch of effects for scoring really high numbers that may trigger epileptic reactions. If you are prone to flashing lights and shaking things, disable some settings in the mod's Effects tab.

Mayhem also has custom operation sounds which get very intense. If your headphones/speakers start distorting and/or the noises are unpleasant, you can disable them in the Audio tab or toggle some less intense versions of those sounds.

# Installation
### [Video installation guide](https://youtu.be/Ldbd94HM8hg?si=YSJAtWxdds9Tr3dq)
Mayhem has the following dependencies which must be installed for the mod to work:
* [Steamodded](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods) - Core modding API, loader and framework
* [Amulet](https://github.com/frostice482/Amulet) - Adds support for *very* large, hyperoperational numbers 
* [Noituus](https://codeberg.org/alex343xd/Noituus) - Stacks identical Consumables and allows you to use most of them in bulk

If this is your first time modding Balatro, check the Wiki on the Steamodded repository for a tutorial on how to set the essentials up.

All of the dependencies on the latest (supported) versions are included in the Dependencies ZIP on every Mayhem release.

Mayhem may conflict with certain content mods. Most popular mods should work, but more obscure and rough mods may cause issues. Cryptid in particular is **NOT** officially supported.

To actually download Mayhem, navigate to the Releases Tab on this repository and download the pre-packaged ZIP (or TAR.GZ) archive containing the source code. 

[Overflow](https://github.com/lord-ruby/Overflow) can be used in place of Noituus, however this is not officially supported. 

# Contents 
Mayhem is big and it grows with each update. Here's a rough overview of what this mod adds:
* The fusion system, which is the quintessential gameplay loop of the mod
* Over 80 Jokers including the fusions, with many more on their way 
	* 5 new rarities for all of the fusions, each one tiering on top of the last
	* Epic Jokers which can appear naturally. Stronger and rarer than Rare Jokers
	* Paradoxical Jokers which are only obtainable from specific mechanics 
* Over 200 Consumables. A surplus of Tarots, Planets and Spectrals alongside:
	* Upside Down versions of all Vanilla Consumables. They can sometimes appear in place of the base Consumable and turn the original Consumable's effect into a downside but with a new, stronger upside 
	* Yotta Cards. They can rarely replace Consumables of certain types and provide strong, usually scalable bonuses with a downside 
	* Retro Cards. They give powerful benefits as long as the player has preformed a specific action a specific number of times. Using a Retro Card will permanently increase the requirement for future copies 
	* Modifier Cards corresponding to nearly all Vanilla and Mayhem modifiers. They give bonuses scaling with the number of cards held in hand with their respective modifier 
	* Miscellaneous Consumables only obtainable from specific sources
* Over 15 Decks 
* Over 50 Vouchers 
	* Plenty of regular Vouchers alongside a 3rd tier for certain Vouchers (more are on their way!)
	* Voucher Bundles which redeem multiple random Vouchers 
	* Special Vouchers which only appear under specific conditions in the shop on top of the existing Vouchers. These are basically meta-upgrades for your run and they unlock some pretty powerful systems and benefits
* Over 10 Enhancements, Editions and exactly 10 Seals 
* Over 20 Booster Packs 
	* Packs for Retro Cards and Modifier Cards 
	* Voucher Packs exclusive to Endless Mode. You can select 1 of up to 2 random Vouchers to be redeemed 
	* Fusion Packs containing base, fusable Jokers 
	* Premium Packs containing playing cards with guaranteed Enhancements, Editions and Seals 
* Over 15 Tags 
* Over 40 Boss Blinds 
	* Tainted versions of most Vanilla Boss Blinds. They appear in Endless Mode if you have defeated the regular counterpart and they pose a much bigger threat 
* 2 Poker Hands 
* MANY new systems and mechanics 
	* Restricting more powerful content to after certain stages of your run. Certain items can only appear in Endless Mode or after obtaining a Transcendent Joker (currently the last fusion tier)
	* Add Dollars and Score to Poker Hands. You earn their Dollars as money before scoring if you play the hand and the Score is applied after scoring on top the points you initially earned 
	* Increase the Chips and Mult Poker Hands gain when they are leveled up 
	* Instability mechanic used by Omniversal Catalyst, a Joker required for many fusions which noticeably increases Blind Sizes while owned 
	* Transcendence effect which *really* makes the game feel like it's breaking once you start scoring very large numbers 
	* Store Vouchers and Booster Packs in your Consumable Slots if they are created there
	* Sell redeemed Vouchers with Reconfigure, a Special Voucher. Their effects will be disabled 
	* Ways to increase your Card Selection Limit so you can select more than 5 playing cards
	* Change the Score Operator between your Chips and Mult to change how Score is calculated 
	* Certain Consumables can be reserved from Booster Packs by default so you can use them later 
	* Much more interaction with Interest, allowing you to change your Interest and Interest Cap and earn significant amount of money from it even in the late game 
	* Reroll prices will start increasing dramatically after the shop has been rerolled one too many times 
	* Blind Sizes will scale up considerably after specific Rounds to keep you on your toes 
* A large and varied soundtrack alongside many new sound effects and ambiance. Tuned and designed to make the lategame feel almost dream-like and ethereal 
* Changes or full reworks to certain Vanilla content to streamline the experience 
* Esoteric references and humor 
* Random, silly fun facts on the loading screen 
* Custom main menu and intro cutscene

# Recommended Mods
Mayhem releases come with a Recommended Mods ZIP which has a plethora of smaller mods that work excellently with Mayhem. These are obviously optional, but they make the experience feel more complete. 

Mods marked with (CM) enable additional content from Mayhem when used.

### QoL
* Handy: General QoL mod that allows you to swipe to select cards, increase the game speed to really high amounts and mass destroy Tags and Consumables in case of emergency
* Cartomancer: Miscellaneous QoL mod that mainly allows you to zoom in or hide your Jokers and Consumables, improves hand card sorting, limit Chip/Mult flames and your deck size
* Galdur: Overhauls the New Run tab to make it more efficient and pleasing when you have a lot of Decks and Stakes 
* Spotlight: Highlights card area changes, like Joker/Consumable Slot and Hand Size increases
* Banner: Allows you to disable individual pieces of content
* BetterTags: Visually stacks identical Tags
* DebugPlus: While intended for mod developers, it is still handy for showing your FPS and allowing you to cheat in cards if you just want to mess around
* Blueprint: Makes the Blueprint and Brainstorm Jokers display the Joker they're copying
* Next Ante Preview: Shows a preview of the Blinds in the next Ante at the cash out screen after defeating a Boss Blind
* Frost's Utils: Optimization and speed utilities 
* UnBlind: Shows a preview of the current ante in the shop instead of the usual Shop sign
* Main Menu Tweaks: Changes the buttons on the main menu, grouping the Options and Mods buttons together
* Lua Patcher: Prevents certain crashes (disable this if you'd like to hunt for bugs!)
### Content
* Engulf (CM): Allows Planet Cards to appear with Editions which are applied to the Poker Hands they affect
* Fitxes: Adds Token consumables which give Tags when used
* Espazio (CM): Adds Blank Consumables which turn into the next consumable of their type that's used while held
* Spectrum API: Adds the Spectrum hand type, which contains 5 different suits at once
* Card Sleeves: Adds Sleeves which are equipped on top of decks, analogous to existing decks
* Finity: Adds Showdown Jokers corresponding to each Finisher Boss Blind, which are obtained after defeating them

# For content creators 
First of all, thank you. Second of all, here's some things you should keep in mind if you want to stream and/or make Mayhem content:
* Disable "White noise during Transcendence" in Mayhem's Effects tab. This setting disables the crazy static during high scores, which can absolutely obliterate your bitrate. 
* Enable "Tamer hyperoperation sounds" in Mayhem's Audio & SFX tab. This setting makes the absolutely crazy sounds that play with really high operations more controlled, as they get really earrape-y when layered. 
* Do NOT use the latest release of Slay the Jokers, as it is incompatible with Mayhem. Make sure to instead download the absolute latest commit. 
* If you've played Mayhem once a while back, you should delete your Mayhem config and start fresh to avoid freak bugs from huge version jumps (your config file is ``%AppData%/balatro/config/mayhem.jkr``). 
* Mayhem has custom save files, no need to back your saves up. 
* Minimal Screen Shake is recommended for Mayhem's effects (~25)
* Please add an epilepsy warning.
